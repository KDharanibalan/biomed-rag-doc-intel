# Translational Research Brief: Muscle Proteostasis, EV Signalling, and Nutritional Intervention

**Document ID:** STUDY-PROT-2026-007  
**Domain:** Skeletal Muscle Biology & Multi-Omics Validation  
**Datasets Referenced:** Internal Quantitative Proteomics (Perseus), Bulk RNA-seq, NASA OSDR-33

## 1. Executive Scientific Summary
Disuse-induced and glucocorticoid-induced skeletal muscle atrophy involves coordinated activation of the ubiquitin-proteasome system (UPS), impaired insulin receptor signalling, and intercellular transfer of catabolic cargo via muscle-derived extracellular vesicles (EVs).

## 2. Proteasome and Transcriptional Regulation
Bulk RNA-seq differential expression analyses across denervation and fasting models demonstrate that proteasome subunit genes (including 20S core and 19S regulatory subunits) are regulated by coordinated activity of multiple transcription factors rather than a single master regulator. Candidate E3 ubiquitin ligases such as MuRF1 (TRIM63) and Atrogin-1 (FBXO32) are significantly upregulated during acute muscle wasting.

## 3. Extracellular Vesicle Cargo and Insulin Signalling
Quantitative proteomics via Perseus comparing EVs from atrophying myotubes against control vesicles identified conserved shifts in metabolic enzymes, structural proteins, and signalling regulators. Functional co-culture assays verified that atrophy-derived EVs autonomously propagate catabolic gene signatures and alter downstream insulin receptor sensitivity in recipient myotubes.

## 4. Nutritional Intervention Outcomes
In preclinical disuse and dexamethasone atrophy models, targeted amino-acid and polyphenol-enriched nutritional supplementation significantly attenuated myotube diameter reduction (by 28% relative to untreated atrophy controls) and lowered MuRF1 transcript expression without inducing cytotoxicity.

## 5. Computational-Experimental Consistency Audit
To prevent false-positive biomarker selection, all RNA-seq and proteomics candidates require orthogonal validation via qPCR and Western blotting across at least n = 3 independent biological replicates before inclusion in downstream predictive ANN/Random Forest biomarker panels.
