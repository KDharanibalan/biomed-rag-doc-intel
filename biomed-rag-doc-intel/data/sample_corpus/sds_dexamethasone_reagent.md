# Safety Data Sheet (SDS): Dexamethasone (Cell Culture Grade)

**Document ID:** SDS-CHEM-DEX-009  
**CAS Number:** 50-02-2  
**Molecular Formula:** C22H29FO5  
**Molecular Weight:** 392.46 g/mol  
**Revision Date:** 2025-11-10

## 1. Product Identification and Intended Use
Dexamethasone is a synthetic glucocorticoid reagent supplied strictly for in vitro cellular assays and preclinical research models (including skeletal muscle atrophy induction in C2C12 myotubes and murine models). Not intended for human clinical administration.

## 2. Hazard Identification (GHS Classification)
- **Reproductive Toxicity:** Category 1B (H360 — May damage fertility or the unborn child).
- **Specific Target Organ Toxicity (Repeated Exposure):** Category 2 (H373 — May cause damage to endocrine and musculoskeletal organs through prolonged or repeated exposure).
- **Signal Word:** DANGER.

## 3. Handling, PPE, and Engineering Controls
Weigh dry powder only inside a certified chemical fume hood or powder containment enclosure. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) includes double nitrile gloves, chemical splash goggles, laboratory coat, and N95/P100 particulate respirator when handling unsolubilized powder.

## 4. Storage and Stability Conditions
Store tightly sealed at 2 C to 8 C, protected from light and moisture. Stock solutions dissolved in sterile DMSO or ethanol may be stored at -20 C for up to 3 months. Avoid incompatible strong oxidizing agents.

## 5. Spill Response and Waste Disposal
Avoid generating dust. Absorb spills with inert material dampened with ethanol and transfer into a labeled hazardous chemical waste container. Dispose of unused reagent and contaminated consumables in compliance with institutional hazardous chemical regulations; do not discharge into municipal drains.
